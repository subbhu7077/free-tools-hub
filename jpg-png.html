Convert JPG images to PNG or PNG images to JPG quickly and easily. Processing happens directly in your browser.

Free JPG to PNG and PNG to JPG Converter

This free image converter lets you convert JPG, JPEG, PNG and supported image files into another format without installing any software.

The conversion is performed in your browser using HTML Canvas. Your selected image is not uploaded to a server by this page.

How to Convert an Image

  • Select a JPG or PNG image.
  • Choose PNG or JPG as the output format.
  • If JPG is selected, choose the desired quality.
  • Tap Convert & Download.
  • Your converted image will be downloaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert JPG to PNG?

Yes. Select your JPG image and choose PNG as the output format.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Select PNG and choose JPG as the output format. Transparent PNG areas are placed on a white background when converted to JPG.

Are my images uploaded?

This tool performs the conversion locally in your browser. Your image is not intentionally uploaded to our server.

Does conversion reduce quality?

PNG conversion is lossless. JPG uses adjustable compression, so lower JPG quality can reduce file size but may also reduce image quality.
